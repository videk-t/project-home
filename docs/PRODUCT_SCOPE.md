# Product scope

V1 serves couples, families, and shared homes with Today, tasks, shopping, calendar, expenses, settlements, bills, and household management. It deliberately excludes chat, social feeds, AI, location, banking, media, and calendar provider integrations.

North star: **Weekly Active Households** — two different signed-in members perform meaningful household actions in seven days. Activation in the first 48 hours is two participants where applicable, three household objects, two shared/completed interactions, and two modules used.
